# Deliverable F — Pilot Lesson Plan (Earliest Beginner Stage, Zero-L1)

## F.1 Pilot scope and success criteria

**Scope:** A0-M1 (First Contact): U1 *Hello and Goodbye* + U2 *Names and the
Alphabet* — 12 lessons + module checkpoint M1-C, fully authored, reviewed,
and validated per Deliverable D. Plus seed database rows for every new item.

**Success criteria (all required to pass the pilot):**

1. A learner with zero English can complete U1–U2 using only the materials
   (pictures, actions, repetition, contrast) — judged by two independent
   reviewer walkthroughs answering from student text alone.
2. Zero English-only violations; zero uninventoried language in tests.
3. All 12 lessons reach `validated`; checkpoint M1-C reaches `ready`.
4. Unknown-language audit: ≥95% of lexical running words in the controlled
   reading/practice are taught **before that use**, including explicitly
   modeled current-lesson words. Report prior-lesson coverage separately;
   L01 begins at 0%, not 95%. Never infer learner retention from coverage.
5. Reviewer-reported confusion points ≤3 per lesson, all repaired or logged
   with a fix plan.

## F.2 How English is taught with zero shared language

Techniques (every pilot lesson uses ≥3):

1. **Picture-first.** Meaning arrives via specified images before any text.
   Each lesson lists exact picture specs (P1, P2…).
2. **Show–point–say.** New word = object/action shown + word spoken/written.
3. **TPR (total physical response).** Imperatives taught through doing
   (stand up, point, open) before reading.
4. **Contrast pairs.** hello↔goodbye, I↔you, a↔an, this↔that — meaning from
   difference, never from translation.
5. **Rote-then-rule.** Chunks first (*I am Anna*), analysis later.
6. **Minimal metalanguage.** No grammar terms at A0-M1 except *letter, word,
   name* taught as vocabulary with pictures.
7. **Repetition with variation.** Same pattern, new names/objects; choral →
   individual → game.
8. **Self-study actions.** Every lesson says exactly what to *do* (point,
   say aloud 3 times, cover and recall), since no teacher explains.

## F.4 Lesson-by-lesson plan (objectives + core activities)

- **U1-L01.** Objectives: greet (hello/hi/goodbye/bye); say *I am + name*.
  Activities: P1 wave-hello/P2 wave-goodbye contrast; name-chain game; Lesson
  Check: match greeting→picture, complete *I am ___*.
- **U1-L02.** Objectives: use *My/Your name is + identity*; ask the complete
  fixed chunk *What is your name?* and respond. All five words are explicitly
  taught. Activities: pictured own/other name badges, speaker reversal,
  hidden-name information gap, trace/copy/personal-name writing. No general
  wh-question system, his/her, or object vocabulary is assumed.
- **U1-L03.** Objectives: respond to *Listen/Say/Point*; use Good
  morning/evening/night at the right time. Activities: sun/moon/evening
  pictures; TPR chain.
- **U1-L04.** Objectives: read 4 supported lines; find known words.
  Activities: big-print dialogue poster; circle-the-word tasks.
- **U1-L05.** Objectives: write name + *I am…*; form A–M; capital I.
  Activities: trace→copy→write ladder; alphabet chant A–M.
- **U1-L06.** Full recall games + test A0-U01-T (10 items, §F.6 pattern).
- **U2-L01.** Objectives: name 4 objects with a/an; point with *This is…*.
  Activities: mystery-bag reveal (real objects); a/an sorting by first sound.
- **U2-L02.** Objectives: singular/plural -s; *This is a… / These are…*.
  Activities: one-vs-many trays; plural chant.
- **U2-L03.** Objectives: follow 6 classroom imperatives; use please/thank
  you/sorry. Activities: Simon-says-style game; politeness role-cards.
- **U2-L04.** Objectives: read short imperatives + labels; complete A–Z.
  Activities: label-the-room walk; alphabet ordering race.
- **U2-L05.** Objectives: spell own name aloud; write 3 labelled sentences.
  Activities: spelling-bee pairs; copy→cover→write.
- **U2-L06.** Full M1 recall + test A0-U02-T; checkpoint M1-C (speak: greet +
  introduce + name 3 objects; write: name + 2 sentences).

## F.5 Worked exemplar: A0-M1-U1-L01 (abridged authoring demo)

> This compressed demo shows the method. The full Phase-2 lesson follows
> `templates/LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` completely (exercises, key, recall, plans).

**Title:** Hello and Goodbye — First Words and Sounds (original 45-minute
architecture estimate; actual draft estimate 50–70 minutes, untrialled).
Visual practice is supplied; pronunciation/listening require a competent
helper until audio exists. This internal demo is not the learner file.

**You can already:** nothing in English — start here. **After this lesson,
you can:** 1) say hello and goodbye; 2) say *I am + your name*.

**Pictures.** P1: smiling girl waving, hand open, sun behind her.
P2: same girl walking away, waving back over her shoulder. P3: a boy pointing
at himself. P4: the boy pointing at the girl.

**Step 1 — HELLO (8 min).** Look at P1. The girl says HELLO. Say it: *hello*
(3 times, aloud). Wave your hand. Again: HELLO! — Now P2. The girl goes.
She says GOODBYE. Say it: *goodbye* (3 times). Wave. Again: GOODBYE!
[Contrast drill: teacher/self-study audio alternates P1/P2; learner says the
word and waves. 10 rounds.]

**Step 2 — HI / BYE (5 min).** Short hello = HI. Short goodbye = BYE. Same
pictures, short words. 8 rounds.

**Step 3 — I AM ANNA (10 min).** P3: the boy points at himself. He says:
*I am Tom.* Point at yourself. Say: *I am* + YOUR name (5 times). P4: the boy
points at the girl. He says: *You are Anna.* Point at P1. Say *You are Anna*
(3 times).

**Step 4 — Say it together (7 min).** Dialogue (point at pictures, say aloud):
A: Hello! I am Tom. — B: Hi! I am Anna. — A: Goodbye, Anna! — B: Bye, Tom!

**Exercises (sample).** 1) Look: P1 or P2? Say the word. (4 items)
2) Complete: *I ___ Tom.* (am) 3) Who says it — hello or goodbye? (4 items)
**Key:** provided with picture references.
**Remember (cover and say):** hello? … goodbye? … I am…? … (8 prompts)
**Checklist:** I can say hello/goodbye ☐; I can say my name ☐.

## F.6 Assessment pattern (pilot)

- Lesson Checks: 6–8 items, ≥70% to continue; for eight whole-point items
  this means 6/8 = 75%. Immediate correction is practice only, not a scored
  retake. A scored retake requires a parallel form per Deliverable D. Missing
  oral/written evidence means not-assessed, not a guessed score.
- Unit tests A0-U01-T/A0-U02-T: 10 items (4 vocabulary recognition, 2 grammar
  completion, 2 listening-response, 1 speaking, 1 writing), 20 min, ≥70%.
- Checkpoint M1-C: oral (greet + introduce + follow 3 imperatives) + written
  (name + 2 sentences), rubric pass/fail with repair tasks.

## F.7 Pilot audit plan

After authoring: full Deliverable D gates 1–7 on all 12 lessons; two fresh
reviewer walkthroughs simulating zero English (reviewer may only use the
student text + pictures); confusion-point log; repair pass; then validation
sign-off. Findings feed the Phase 3 revision of templates and procedures.
