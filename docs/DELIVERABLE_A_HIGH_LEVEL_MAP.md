## A.2 Level profiles

### A0 — Starter (Pre-A1)

- **Entry:** no English required. Ability to see pictures and hear/repeat sounds.
- **Target:** understand and use everyday expressions and very basic phrases;
  introduce self and others; ask/answer simple personal questions; read and
  write isolated words and short supported sentences.
- **Communicative abilities:** greetings; names; classroom language; numbers
  1–100; colors; family; common objects; likes; days/time; feelings; simple
  routines and descriptions.
- **Vocabulary:** 350 highest-frequency concrete items (people, things,
  actions, food, places, time). Selection: frequency + concreteness +
  classroom utility.
- **Grammar:** G001–G020 (alphabet; BE; a/an; plurals; this/that; pronouns;
  present simple; imperatives; basic prepositions).
- **Pronunciation:** English alphabet sounds; key consonant/vowel contrasts;
  word stress on known words; clear slowly-spoken modelling.
- **Skills:** listen for words/phrases; speak in words and short sentences;
  read short supported lines; copy and write known words/sentences.
- **Assessment:** 8 unit tests, 4 module checkpoints, A0 level test (A0-LT).
- **Progression:** score ≥70% on A0-LT with all unit tests passed (retakes
  allowed after remediation); demonstrate greetings/introductions orally.

### A1 — Beginner

- **Entry:** A0-LT passed (or diagnostic placement).
- **Target:** CEFR A1 — communicate in simple routine tasks; describe home,
  people, routines, past weekends, plans; handle shopping, directions, simple
  travel needs.
- **Vocabulary:** +650 (cumulative 1,000). Concrete everyday domains; first
  common verbs/adjectives/adverbs; time expressions.
- **Grammar:** G021–G040 (there is/are; some/any; countability; possessives;
  frequency; past simple; can; going to; like+-ing; comparatives/superlatives;
  want/would like).
- **Pronunciation:** full vowel/consonant inventory receptively; -s/-ed
  endings; sentence stress basics; polite intonation.
- **Skills:** short conversations; paragraphs of 40–80 words; short notices,
  menus, timetables; slow clear announcements.
- **Assessment:** 12 unit tests, 6 module checkpoints, A1 level test.
- **Progression:** A1-LT ≥70%; all module checkpoints passed; writing sample
  meets A1 rubric.

### A2 — Elementary

- **Entry:** A1-LT passed.
- **Target:** CEFR A2 — handle short social exchanges; describe experiences,
  journeys, jobs, plans, rules, opinions; narrate past events; write linked
  sentences and short letters/messages.
- **Vocabulary:** +1,000 (cumulative 2,000). Travel, work, study, communication,
  feelings; first phrasal verbs (10–15); common collocations.
- **Grammar:** G041–G060 (continuous aspects; past narrative contrast; future
  system; obligation/advice modals; present perfect introduction; conditionals
  type 1; gerund/infinitive basics; comparison system).
- **Pronunciation:** weak forms; connected speech awareness; past-tense
  endings; intonation for lists, questions, feelings.
- **Skills:** 1–2 minute turns; 80–120 word texts; everyday signs, emails,
  simple stories; announcements and slow conversations.
- **Assessment:** 12 unit tests, 6 module checkpoints, A2 level test,
  cumulative A0–A2 assessment (CUM-A2).
- **Progression:** A2-LT ≥70% + CUM-A2 ≥65%; recorded speaking sample passes.

### B1 — Intermediate (independent user begins)

- **Entry:** A2-LT + CUM-A2 passed.
- **Target:** CEFR B1 — deal with most travel situations; describe experiences,
  ambitions, opinions with reasons; narrate and report; write connected text
  on familiar topics.
- **Vocabulary:** +1,500 (cumulative 3,500). Abstract nouns (reasons, results,
  opinions); work/media/health/environment domains; 60+ phrasal verbs;
  discourse markers.
- **Grammar:** G061–G080 (perfect/continuous integration; past perfect; used
  to; 2nd conditional; passive introduction; reported speech; relatives;
  possibility modals; future system completion; connectors).
- **Pronunciation:** stress-timing; thought groups; contrastive stress;
  reductions in natural speech; repair strategies.
- **Skills:** 2–3 minute presentations; 120–180 word essays/letters; news,
  grievances, instructions; main ideas of clear standard speech.
- **Assessment:** 16 unit tests, 8 module checkpoints, B1 level test.
- **Progression:** B1-LT ≥70%; writing portfolio (4 pieces) meets B1 rubric.

### B2 — Upper-Intermediate

- **Entry:** B1-LT passed.
- **Target:** CEFR B2 — interact fluently with native speakers; argue with
  relevant reasons; understand standard speech and complex text lines of
  argument; write clear detailed text.
- **Vocabulary:** +1,500 (cumulative 5,000). Academic-general words; society,
  science, media, psychology domains; precise synonyms; register awareness;
  120+ phrasal verbs total.
- **Grammar:** G081–G090 (3rd/mixed conditionals; wish; full passive system;
  advanced reported/relative/verb-pattern grammar; participles; clefts).
  Grammar instruction shifts toward accuracy-in-use over new forms.
- **Pronunciation:** intonation for attitude and emphasis; chunking long
  turns; self-monitoring and self-correction.
- **Skills:** sustained discussion; 180–250 word structured essays; articles,
  reports, reviews; lectures with clear structure.
- **Assessment:** 16 unit tests, 8 module checkpoints, B2 level test,
  cumulative A0–B2 assessment (CUM-B2).
- **Progression:** B2-LT ≥70% + CUM-B2 ≥65%; discussion performance passes.

### C1 — Advanced

- **Entry:** B2-LT + CUM-B2 passed.
- **Target:** CEFR C1 — understand demanding long texts; express ideas
  fluently without searching; use language flexibly for academic purposes;
  produce well-structured detailed text with controlled cohesion.
- **Vocabulary:** +2,000 (cumulative 7,000). Academic Word List coverage;
  nominalization; hedging/caution language; field-specific academic lexis;
  fine synonym/register distinctions.
- **Grammar:** G091–G100 (inversion; fronting; hedging; complex subordination;
  nominalization; ellipsis; phrasal-verb grammar; advanced reference;
  formal frames; cohesion systems).
- **Pronunciation:** near-natural rhythm/intonation; presentation delivery;
  accent intelligibility across varieties (not accent elimination).
- **Skills:** seminar participation; data description; thesis-driven essays;
  dense articles; full lectures with note-taking.
- **Assessment:** 16 unit tests, 8 module checkpoints, C1 level test,
  cumulative A0–C1 assessment (CUM-C1).
- **Progression:** C1-LT ≥70% + CUM-C1 ≥65%; portfolio + presentation pass.
  This qualifies entry to IELTS preparation.

### IE — IELTS Academic Preparation

- **Entry:** C1 level test passed (B2+ minimum with a bridging plan if a
  learner enters here directly — see Deliverable B, §B.8).
- **Target:** exam readiness — complete all four components under timed
  conditions with stable estimated band performance; no tricks substituting
  for proficiency.
- **Coverage:** Listening (4 parts, 40 Qs, ~30 min); Reading (3 passages,
  40 Qs, 60 min); Writing (Task 1 ≥150 words ~20 min; Task 2 ≥250 words
  ~40 min); Speaking (3 parts, 11–14 min). Format verified against official
  sources in October 2026 (see INITIAL_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md); re-verify in
  Phase 6 before publishing mocks.
- **Vocabulary:** +800 receptive academic/collocational items; paraphrase
  banks; topic lexicons (environment, education, technology, health, society).
- **Assessment:** diagnostic test, per-unit checkpoints, 2 full mock tests
  (original material, clearly labelled as such), final readiness review.
- **Integrity:** no fabricated official questions; no guaranteed band scores;
  AI/human-estimated scores labelled as estimates. See Deliverable D, §D.7.

## A.4 Review and assessment spine

Every unit ends with a review lesson + unit test (98 total). Every module ends
with a checkpoint (46). Every level ends with a level test (6) plus cumulative
assessments at A2/B2/C1 (3). IELTS adds a diagnostic, 18 checkpoints, and 2
full mocks. Spaced retrieval (+1/+3/+7/+21/+60 days, adaptive) runs inside
every lesson's Review Plan (template §19). Full framework: ASSESSMENT_REGISTER.md.
